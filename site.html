With a distinguished 42-year tenure/21-years of sea time in the Maritime and Offshore Industries, I am a seasoned Master Mariner and Offshore Installation Manager, having commanded ships and managed offshore installations with unparalleled expertise. My career, started as a Deck Cadet in 1980 and progressing to Captain in 2002, has been marked by leadership in Maritime Transport and the Oil and Gas sectors. My comprehensive skill set spans versatile leadership, Marine Engineering, Nautical Navigation, Maritime Operations, Environmental Compliance, Oil and Gas Logistics, Global Logistics, maritime compliance, Port Operations, Safety Operations, Crew Management, Organisational Development, Personal Development, Digital Proficiency, and Strategic Business Management. I excel in life extension studies, mooring system, engineering evaluations, and ensuring compliance with industry standards and classification society requirements, coupled with my expertise in operational support for floating units, positions me as a strategic leader adept at enhancing marine project efficiency and safety.

I excel in adaptability, analytical thinking, Conflict Resolution, and communication, fostering Teamwork and building relationships that enhance operational efficiency. My deep knowledge of Maritime Industry Standards, marine operations, seamanship, Maritime Law, and International Regulations ensures the highest levels of maritime safety and security. I have mastered advanced nautical navigation, ensuring proficiency in ECDIS, GMDSS, and radar operations for safe and efficient voyage planning.

My expertise in maritime operations and environmental compliance is evident in my handling of tanker ships, LNG Carriers, and Container Ships and Bulk Carriers, adhering to Emissions Trading schemes. In the oil and gas sector, I have led cargo operations, Quality System Management, and Stakeholder Engagement, ensuring effective logistics and supply chain optimisation.

I possess a robust understanding of maritime compliance, Project Integrity Management, and port operations, combined with legislative interfacing, to maintain operational excellence. My commitment to marine and industrial safety operations is demonstrated through adherence to HSEQ standards and emergency management protocols.

As a leader, I prioritize crew welfare and development, alongside crew management, focusing on Talent Management and Employee Engagement. My strategic approach to organisational development and strategy implementation has driven business growth and operational transformation.
