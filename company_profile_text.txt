Executive Summary
STEVE JOHNSON INTEGRATED SERVICES LTD.
is a specialist marine and offshore consulting firm providing high‑level marine assurance, regulatory compliance support, and operational advisory services to vessel operators, offshore energy companies, and maritime organizations worldwide.
Led by
Capt. Steve Johnson — Master Mariner (Unlimited), Offshore Installation Manager, and Flag-State Nautical Inspector (RMI)
— the company delivers expert oversight across FSO/FPSO operations, tanker vetting, SIRE 2.0 readiness, emergency management, and statutory compliance.
With 42+ years of global maritime experience, including command of VLCC-class offshore assets and extensive regulatory inspection work for the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI), the company offers unmatched operational insight, safety leadership, and digital assurance capability.
We help clients achieve
safe operations, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence
across high‑hazard marine and offshore environments.
Vission & Mission Statement
Vision
To be a trusted global partner in marine assurance and offshore operational integrity, recognized for excellence, professionalism, and unwavering commitment to safety.
Mission
To enhance marine and offshore operational safety through expert assurance, regulatory precision, and disciplined leadership – delivering reliable, compliant, and high‑performance outcomes for clients worldwide.
