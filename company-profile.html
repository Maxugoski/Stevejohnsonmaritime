Executive Summary

World-Class Marine Assurance & Technical Precision

STEVE JOHNSON INTEGRATED SERVICES LTD. is a specialist marine and offshore consulting firm providing high‑level marine assurance, regulatory compliance support, and operational advisory services to vessel operators, offshore energy companies, and maritime organizations worldwide.

Led by Capt. Steve Johnson — Master Mariner (Unlimited), Offshore Installation Manager (OIM), and Flag-State Nautical Inspector (Republic of the Marshall Islands) — the company delivers expert oversight across FSO/FPSO operations, tanker vetting, SIRE 2.0 readiness, emergency management, and statutory compliance.

With 42+ years of global maritime experience, including command of VLCC-class offshore assets (ExxonMobil Yoho FSO) and extensive regulatory inspection work for the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI), the company offers unmatched operational insight, safety leadership, and digital assurance capability.

We help clients achieve safe operations, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence across high‑hazard marine and offshore environments.
